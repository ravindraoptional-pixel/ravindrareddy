About

Who I am

I trained in computer science and information technology — a B.Tech in CSIT — and that is still the foundation under the work. I know how a system is put together. These days I am more interested in the systems organisations run around people: hiring, payroll, documentation, compliance, and the small operational problems that decide whether a process actually works.

On the current site I describe it more plainly. HR professional by profession, writer by passion. Talent acquisition is where I learn how people present themselves. Writing is where I try to make sense of the world around them. The same instinct shows up in recruitment, in a payroll checklist, and in a page of notes: take something messy and hand it back in a shape someone can use.

Along the way I have kept a blog, written articles, designed, and experimented with a Telugu and Tenglish YouTube channel on self-development and current affairs. Different rooms, same habit. I like building useful things, and I am still early enough in the HR and operations direction to say that out loud.

When life gives you lemons, give them back and ask coffee.A line from the current site
